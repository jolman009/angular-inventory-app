1. Information We Collect and Store

{{ brand.name }} collects only the minimum information required to authenticate your account and maintain your personal collection catalog:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and an encrypted password hash managed by Supabase Authentication.
  • Collection Catalog Data: We store the items you add or import into your vault, including titles, media categories (cards, books, games, vinyl records), ownership status (owned, wishlist, for sale, sold, lent out), condition, purchase prices, collector notes, category-specific fields (such as year, set, grade, ISBN, publisher, or format), and saved eBay market valuation snapshots.
  • Uploaded Photos: When you attach a photo to an item or use AI Quick Capture, the image is resized in your browser and stored in a private cloud storage bucket (item-images) scoped to your unique user ID.
  • Local Device Preferences: We store your authentication session token and display theme preference (vaulted-theme: system, light, or dark) in your browser's local storage.

2. How We Use Your Information & Third-Party Processors

We use your stored information strictly to operate and improve {{ brand.name }}:

  • Cloud Database & Storage (Supabase): Your account credentials, collection records, and uploaded photos are hosted on Supabase infrastructure protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), ensuring only your authenticated session can access your records.
  • AI Quick Capture (Google Gemini Vision): When you scan an item photo for automatic field suggestions, the image is transmitted securely to our scan-item edge function and processed by the Google Gemini Vision API to extract visible text and metadata. Images sent for scanning are used solely to return suggestions to you.
  • Card Market Valuations (eBay Browse API): When you request a card price estimate, non-personal card attributes (player/title, year, set, card number, grade, and sport) are sent to our ebay-price edge function to calculate median listing prices from eBay. Your email address and account identity are never shared with eBay.
Account & Data Erasure

3. How to Delete Your Account and Stored Data

You have complete control over your data and may delete individual items or permanently delete your entire {{ brand.name }} account and all associated stored data at any time.

Option A: Self-Service Account & Data Deletion in the App

  1. Sign in to your {{ brand.name }} account and navigate to My Collection.
  2. Optional: Click Export in the collection toolbar (or inside the mobile Import/Export menu) to download a complete CSV backup of your catalog before deleting your account.
  3. On desktop, locate the bottom of the left navigation sidebar and click Delete account & data. On mobile or tablet, tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar and tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. {{ brand.name }} will immediately delete all of your cataloged items and photo drafts from the database, purge all of your uploaded photos from the item-images storage bucket, clear your local browser preferences, and sign you out.

Option B: Deleting Individual Items or Photos

To remove specific records without closing your account, click Delete on any item in your collection list or inside the Item Inspector drawer. Deleting an item immediately deletes both its database record and its associated photo file from cloud storage.

Option C: Requesting Account & Data Deletion via Email

If you are unable to sign in to your account or wish to request complete removal of your authentication record and all stored data through support, send an email from your registered email address to {{ brand.supportEmail }} with the subject line "Delete My {{ brand.name }} Account". We will permanently erase your authentication profile, database records, and stored images within 7 business days (and never later than 30 days) and send you a final confirmation once erasure is complete.

What is permanently removed when you delete your account:
  • All cataloged items, drafts, notes, purchase prices, and eBay valuation data
  • All item photos uploaded to the private item-images storage bucket
  • Your local theme and session data stored in your browser
  • Your account login credentials and authentication profile

4. Data Retention & Portability

We retain your collection data only for as long as your account remains active. You can export your entire collection to a standard CSV file at any time using the in-app Export feature. Once you delete an item or delete your account, the data cannot be recovered.
